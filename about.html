OUR STORY

Built From A Simple Belief:
Every Senior Deserves A Meaningful Day.

ElderCare began with a deeply personal observation: while medical care for seniors was widely available, day-to-day companionship, joyful intellectual stimulation, and uplifting community spaces were rarely accessible for families balancing busy professional careers.

In 2019, our founders set out to build an environment that felt nothing like a traditional clinical institution, and everything like a welcoming second home. We designed sunlit communal lounges, gentle wellness studios, organic gardens, and quiet reflection spaces where seniors could thrive together.

Today, ElderCare has grown into a cherished sanctuary for hundreds of senior citizens and their loved ones. Every activity, meal, and conversation is crafted to cultivate happiness, self-worth, and lasting peace of mind.

OUR JOURNEY

Milestones Along
Our Path of Care.

2019

The Beginning

ElderCare was founded with a mission to create a daytime community where seniors thrive with dignity, active engagement, and dedicated healthcare oversight.

2021

Growing Community

Expanded our activity facilities, welcoming over 100 families and adding custom sensory relaxation lounges and hydrotherapy wellness suites.

2023

Comprehensive Wellness

Introduced structured physiotherapy, memory-enrichment workshops, music therapy, and customized daily geriatric dietary plans.
